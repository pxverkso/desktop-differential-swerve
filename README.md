## General Information 
This Desktop Differential Drive is a display piece with a real size differetial swerve with knobs to simulate real power delivery to the wheel acceleration and steering.

## Why did I build this?
I built this display piece to go with my Co-Axial Swerve Drive Display but also to learn about differentials and building a swerve drive out of it. I also designed this to upload and open source for other swerve drive enthusiasts to build and put on their desk. Whichever color scheme you go for, it'll look beautiful. 

## How does it work? 
The differential swerve drive is built on 2 bevel gears that also have teeth extending outwards. The pinion bevel is connected to the wheel. When 1 bevel gear spins, the wheel turns and also steers. When both bevels spin in the same direction, the wheel stays still and then steers. When both bevels spin but are inverted, just the wheel turns. By utilizing this, in a non-display application, a Differential swerve drive could be used as a holonomic drive for navigating in any direction.
